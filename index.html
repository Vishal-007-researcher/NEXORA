About Nexora

Rainfall prediction prototype

Data source: Open-Meteo (live weather and daily history from 2021 to 2026).

Model: Logistic regression. It gives a risk score for whether more than 1 mm of rain will fall tomorrow. It uses today's weather, the last 3 and 7 days of rainfall, and pressure and humidity changes.

Test results: Accuracy is about 70%. The model catches about 74% of rainy days (recall). The baseline, which always predicts "no rain", scores 63%.

Risk score: To avoid missing rainy days, the model gives extra weight to them during training. As a result, the score is usually higher than a true probability.

Backend: Python and Flask. Login uses user accounts, and passwords are stored as hashes.

Prototype only. Please use the IMD for official forecasts.
